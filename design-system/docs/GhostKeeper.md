The Tool Shed's shopkeeper: a friendly ghost with spectacles, a mustache, a bowler hat and a leather apron. Its hands rest on the counter.

```jsx
<GhostKeeper width={480} height={520} yaw={14} pitch={6} floating />
```

- `floating` makes it bob up and down, the way it floats behind the counter.
- `counter` shows the shed's counter in front of it. It's only on the board.
- It takes the stage's camera props too, like `yaw`, `pitch`, `spin` and `person`.
