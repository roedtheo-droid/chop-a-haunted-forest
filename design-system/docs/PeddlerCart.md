A peddler's hand cart with a striped canvas roof whose edge looks like teeth. It stands beside every sell pad in the game and opens the SHOP menu.

```jsx
<PeddlerCart width={640} height={480} yaw={24} pitch={10} person />
```

- On it are a fat sack, two stacks of coins, crates, a lantern and a bell.
- `words` shows a bar on the hanging sign, where the game writes the real word. It's only on the board.
- It takes the stage's camera props too, like `yaw`, `pitch`, `spin` and `person`.
